CodeRa vs Google Lens for extracting code

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Google Lens is general OCR; CodeRa is a Chrome workflow for tutorial code. Use Lens for signs and casual text. Use CodeRa when you need indentation preserved and a one-click capture from YouTube or Udemy.
