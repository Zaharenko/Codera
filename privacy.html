Privacy Policy

This policy explains how CodeRa, a Chrome extension built by Anton Zakharenka, handles your data when you capture screenshots or video frames, collect a lesson, store snippets locally, or use optional exports and AI follow-ups.

Effective date: October 8, 2026 Extension version: 1.2.0 Chrome Web Store
Short version: CodeRa does not operate a cloud library of your screenshots. When you extract (including many frames during “Collect whole lesson”), images and related prompts are sent to Anthropic’s API under Anthropic’s privacy policy. Snippets you keep live in local browser storage on your device unless you export them yourself.

1. Who operates CodeRa

CodeRa is developed and maintained by Anton Zakharenka (“we”, “us”). CodeRa is an independent product and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or developed by Anthropic.

Contact: anton.zaharenko@gmail.com (domain mailbox privacy@codera.click may be added later; until then use this address).

2. Data processed when you use the extension

When you trigger a capture, upload, lesson collection, follow-up chat, or language conversion, CodeRa may process:

  • Screenshots or video frames from area selection, video frame capture (including Alt+C / floating button), uploaded image files (PNG, JPEG, WebP), or frames sampled during Collect whole lesson
  • Visible page content in the captured region (code, text, subtitles, UI)
  • Snippet text you already extracted, when you use follow-up chat, “explain”, or language conversion — sent as text to Anthropic’s API for that request
  • Your selected extraction mode (plain text, extract as code, understand)
  • Browser UI language for localized explanations when applicable
  • Page URL / title metadata used locally to label snippets in your library (for example YouTube or Udemy source filters)

Image and text payloads for AI features are transmitted to Anthropic’s API (Anthropic Privacy Policy) solely to perform the action you requested. We do not use this data for advertising or profiling.

Collect whole lesson: one session may send many frames to Anthropic as you watch or scrub. Only start collection on content you are comfortable sending to an AI API.

3. Data stored locally on your device

CodeRa uses Chrome storage (including chrome.storage.local / IndexedDB as implemented) to save:

  • Preferred extraction mode and UI preferences
  • Optional Anthropic API key, if you enter one in settings
  • Snippet library — extracted text/code, timestamps, source labels, and related metadata you choose to keep
  • Lesson-collection state (in-progress frames / assembled packs) until you clear or export them

This information stays on your device. CodeRa does not sync the library to a CodeRa-operated cloud database. Uninstalling the extension or clearing extension data removes local storage.

4. What we do not collect

  • We do not maintain a screenshot library on CodeRa servers
  • We do not require a CodeRa account to use the extension
  • We do not sell your personal data
  • We do not use advertising trackers inside the extension
  • We do not continuously monitor browsing — processing happens when you capture, collect a lesson, chat about a snippet, convert languages, or export

5. Chrome permissions explained

CodeRa requests permissions for core functionality:

  • activeTab — access the current tab when you start a capture
  • scripting — inject content scripts for area selection, floating button, hotkey UI, toasts, and lesson-collection helpers on pages you use
  • clipboardWrite — copy extracted text or code (and editor-bridge payloads) to the clipboard
  • storage — mode preference, optional API key, and local snippet / lesson data
  • Host access (<all_urls>) — run where you choose to extract (tutorials, docs, courses)
  • api.anthropic.com (and any documented CodeRa proxy host, if enabled) — send captures / prompts for AI processing

6. Third-party services

Anthropic (Claude Vision / text API)

Images and snippet text for AI features are sent to Anthropic. Their handling is governed by anthropic.com/privacy. Early-access builds may route requests with a shared developer key or your own key; a limited proxy may be introduced later — if so, this section will describe what the proxy sees (request payloads) and that it is not used to build a CodeRa screenshot archive.

Optional exports you initiate

If you export to GitLab Snippets, Notion, Obsidian, Anki, or similar tools, snippet content leaves your browser according to your action and those products’ policies. CodeRa does not upload exports automatically.

VS Code / Cursor companion

The editor bridge copies a marked snippet to the clipboard for a local companion command (“CodeRa: Insert Snippet”). That flow stays on your machine unless you paste elsewhere.

Website waitlist (optional)

Emails submitted on the landing waitlist are handled by FormSubmit and delivered to the developer’s inbox. Separate from normal extension use.

7. The codera.click website

The marketing site may use standard server logs (IP, user-agent, requested URL) for security and reliability. If privacy-friendly analytics (for example Plausible or Umami) are enabled later, they will be disclosed here. The site does not require an account. Waitlist email is optional (see above).

8. Data retention

CodeRa does not retain captured images in a CodeRa cloud after processing. Local preferences and your snippet library remain until you delete them, clear extension data, or uninstall.

Data processed by Anthropic is subject to Anthropic’s retention practices. We do not control how long Anthropic retains API request data.

9. Security

Captures are transmitted over HTTPS. Avoid capturing credentials, private keys, or confidential material you would not share with an AI API. You are responsible for complying with the terms of platforms whose content you capture (see also Terms of Use).

10. Children’s privacy

CodeRa is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Your choices and rights

  • Stop processing — uninstall or do not trigger captures / lesson collect / AI follow-ups
  • Clear local data — remove CodeRa data in Chrome extension settings
  • Use your own API key — when available in settings, use your Anthropic key instead of any shared early-access key
  • Contact us — privacy questions or waitlist deletion requests via email below

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the extension or legal requirements change. The “Effective date” at the top will be revised. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
