Privacy Policy

This policy explains how CodeRa, a Chrome extension built by Anton Zakharenka, handles your data when you capture screenshots or video frames, collect a lesson, store snippets locally, or use optional exports and AI follow-ups.

Effective date: October 8, 2026 Features: see changelog Chrome Web Store
Short version: CodeRa does not keep a cloud screenshot library for you. When you extract (including many frames during “Collect whole lesson”), the extension posts imageData, extractionMode, and locale to a CodeRa relay at https://codera.click/api/extract, which forwards the request to Anthropic. Your snippet library stays in local browser storage unless you export it.

1. Who operates CodeRa

CodeRa is developed and maintained by Anton Zakharenka (“we”, “us”), an independent developer. Contact: anton.zaharenko@gmail.com. CodeRa is not affiliated with Anthropic.

2. What is sent for AI extraction (verified in extension code)

What is sent to the CodeRa relay / Anthropic. When you trigger an action, the extension sends to https://codera.click/api/extract: (a) the screenshot or video frame(s) as imageData (PNG/JPEG/WebP data URL); (b) extractionMode (plain text, extract as code, or understand); (c) locale (browser UI language). In “Collect whole lesson”, multiple frames are sent while collection is active. For follow-up questions and language conversion, the extension renders the prompt (snippet text + your question or target language) into an image and sends that image the same way. Lecture-summary prompts may include a page title inside that rendered image when you run summary from the library.

Not included in the JSON body today: page URL, cookies, or browsing history are not fields on the extract request. URL/title/timestamp are used locally to label snippets in your library. Prefer selecting only the code region so other windows or personal UI are not in the frame.

Function Sent via relay
Frame / area / upload Image + mode + locale
Understand mode Same (mode selects explain-style processing)
Follow-up / convert language Rendered prompt image (code + question/target)
Collect whole lesson Many frames while collection is on
Bracket check, library search, filters Nothing (local only)
Export GitLab / Notion / Obsidian / Anki To that service when you press export — not via Anthropic

3. Who processes it (relay + Anthropic)

Requests go first to the CodeRa extract endpoint on codera.click (relay), then to Anthropic under Anthropic’s commercial terms and privacy documentation. The relay exists to run extraction without embedding a user-facing Anthropic key in every call. We do not use extract payloads to build a personal screenshot archive or for advertising.

Under Anthropic’s current Commercial Terms, Anthropic does not train models on API customer content by default; retention is defined by Anthropic. See Anthropic’s current documentation and Privacy Policy — we do not quote a fixed retention day count here because it may change.

Your IP address is visible to the relay host; Anthropic may see the relay’s egress IP rather than yours, depending on infrastructure. International transfer: Anthropic processes data in the United States (and other regions they document). Review their DPA / terms for details.

4. Local storage

Your snippet library, history, settings, optional API key (if you add one), and export tokens (for example GitLab) are stored in your browser (chrome.storage.local / IndexedDB) and are not uploaded to a CodeRa cloud library. Removing the extension deletes them.

5. What we do not collect

  • No CodeRa account is required
  • We do not sell personal data
  • No advertising trackers inside the extension
  • No continuous browsing surveillance — processing runs when you capture, collect a lesson, ask follow-ups, convert language, or export

6. Exports

Exports to GitLab Snippets, Notion, Obsidian and Anki happen only when you press the export button. For services that require sign-in, access tokens are stored locally. Content you export is governed by that service’s own terms.

7. Chrome permissions

  • activeTab — current tab when you start a capture
  • scripting — area selection, floating button, hotkey UI, toasts, lesson helpers
  • clipboardWrite — copy results (and marked clipboard payloads for editors)
  • storage — preferences, optional keys/tokens, local library
  • Host access — pages where you choose to extract; codera.click for the extract relay and site pages

8. Legal basis and your rights (GDPR where applicable)

We process extract requests to perform the function you request (contractual necessity / legitimate interest in operating the product you installed). Waitlist email is based on your consent; you may ask for deletion anytime. Where GDPR applies you may have rights of access, erasure, restriction, and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Contact us at the email below to exercise rights related to waitlist data or questions about the relay.

9. Waitlist

If you submit your email, it is delivered through FormSubmit to the developer’s inbox, used only for product updates and pricing announcements. Ask for deletion at any time via email.

10. The codera.click website

The site and extract relay may produce standard server logs (IP, user-agent, URL, time) for security and reliability. If cookieless analytics (Plausible/Umami) are enabled, they will be named here. No account is required to browse the marketing pages.

11. Data retention

We do not operate a user-facing cloud library of your screenshots. Local data remains until you clear it or uninstall. Anthropic retention follows Anthropic’s then-current terms. Relay logs, if any, are kept only as needed for abuse prevention and operations.

12. Security

Traffic uses HTTPS. Avoid capturing secrets. Prefer cropping to code-only regions. You must comply with platform terms for content you capture (Terms of Use).

13. Children’s privacy

CodeRa is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

14. Your choices

  • Stop using capture / lesson collect / AI follow-ups, or uninstall
  • Clear extension storage in Chrome
  • Use your own Anthropic API key in settings when that option is available
  • Email us for waitlist deletion or privacy questions

15. Changes

We may update this policy when the product or law changes. The effective date above will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy. Feature history: changelog.
