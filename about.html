Hi, I’m Anton Zakharenka, an independent developer and the person behind CodeRa. I build tools for people who learn programming from video — the same problem that made me pause tutorials one too many times.

Why CodeRa exists

I kept pausing tutorials, squinting at the screen and retyping code, with broken indentation and a lot of wasted time. CodeRa is the tool I wanted: capture the frame, get formatted code, keep a local library, and, if you like, collect a whole lesson into a project.

What CodeRa does

  • Captures a video frame with Alt+C or the floating button, without leaving the lesson
  • Extracts code, plain text, or an explanation of what the code does
  • Collects a whole lesson into a timestamped timeline and project-style files (1.2.0, rolling out)
  • Keeps a local snippet library and exports to Obsidian, Notion Markdown, Anki and GitLab Snippets (1.2.0, rolling out)

How I try to build it

  • Honest comparisons. Our comparison pages say where other tools are the better choice.
  • Clear privacy. No account, no cloud library, and a plain-language description of exactly what is sent. See the Privacy Policy.
  • No invented proof. No made-up reviews or statistics. When there are real numbers, they will be published with their method.
  • Feedback shapes the roadmap. See the roadmap.

Independence
