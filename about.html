Why it exists

I kept pausing tutorials, squinting at code, and retyping it into the editor — with broken indentation and wasted time. CodeRa is the tool I wanted: capture the frame, get formatted code, keep a local library, and optionally collect a whole lesson into a project pack.

What CodeRa does

  • Capture a video frame (Alt+C or floating button) without leaving the lesson
  • Extract as code, plain text, or get an explanation
  • Collect a whole lesson while you watch, then export timeline + HTML/CSS/JS
  • Local snippet library; optional exports to Obsidian, Anki, Notion, GitLab Snippets
  • Marked clipboard copy for pasting into any editor

Principles

  • No CodeRa account required
  • Honest comparisons (including offline tools)
  • Clear privacy: relay + Anthropic, local library — see Privacy

Independence

CodeRa is not affiliated with Anthropic. Extraction uses a CodeRa relay and Anthropic’s API — details in Privacy and Terms.
