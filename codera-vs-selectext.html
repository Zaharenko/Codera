CodeRa vs Selectext

By Anton Zakharenka · Checked on 8 Oct 2026 from public product descriptions · Not a paid partnership

Short answer: Selectext focuses on copying text from course videos. CodeRa is built for developers who need formatted code, a whole-lesson pack and a local library. Choose Selectext if you only need plain text and like its account flow. Choose CodeRa if you want extract-as-code, lesson collection, exports and syntax checks.

When Selectext may be enough

  • You need captions or plain text from a video, not paste-ready source.
  • You want Firefox support today.
  • You already have its account flow and are happy with it.

When CodeRa fits better

  • Indentation and runnable snippets matter.
  • You want to collect a whole lecture into project files.
  • You want a library, exports and a bracket check without leaving Chrome.
  • You prefer not to sign in.
Fair warning: CodeRa sends images to a cloud AI service. If that is a problem for your code, see offline alternatives.
